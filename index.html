About me

Student, builder,
curious about everything.

Hi, I'm Huy, a freshman at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT). My passion for math and technology was sparked during my high school years. I started programming and building my first projects back then, and earning a few small awards along the way gave me the confidence that I was on the right track. I thrive on learning new things and building solutions from scratch, and I always bring full enthusiasm and dedication to every project I undertake.

Outside of tech, I believe in cultivating a balanced and multi-dimensional life. I enjoy playing the guitar and piano, which not only nurture my creativity and patience but also teach me how to listen and harmonize with others. I'm also deeply into sports, particularly badminton and football. Whether on the court or the pitch, these activities build my discipline and strategic thinking, while giving me wonderful opportunities to connect with people from all walks of life.

I believe the best tech solutions come from a blend of logical algorithms and human connection. As I continue my journey at HCMUT, I'm eager to build things that are not only technically sound but also bring real value to people.
